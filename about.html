The story behind the guide

A calmer way
to travel

S'évader — French for “to escape” — is a pocket travel companion built for people who'd rather enjoy the trip than manage it.

Between hotel confirmations, currency math, weather surprises, and a dozen browser tabs of "best things to do," trip planning can quietly take over the trip itself. S'évader keeps everything you actually need in one calm, uncluttered place — so the only thing left to do is go.
