S'évader

Travel itinerary app

S'évader helps you plan and carry a trip in one calm place: city notes, practical blocks for hotels and flights, quick context like weather and currency, and lists of sights with entrance fees so you can budget before you go.

Your edits can be saved in the browser so drafts survive between sessions. Rates and fees are indicative—always double-check official sites before you book.

The name S'évader is about slipping away: a small escape, thoughtfully mapped.
